What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack in which criminals pretend to be trusted people or organizations to trick users into sharing passwords, banking details, or personal information.

Common Types of Phishing

  • Email Phishing: Fake emails asking for sensitive information.
  • SMS Phishing: Fraudulent text messages with suspicious links.
  • Website Phishing: Fake websites designed to look genuine.
  • Spear Phishing: Targeted messages designed for a particular person.
  • Social Engineering: Manipulating people into unsafe actions.

Warning Signs of Phishing

Suspicious Links

Check website addresses carefully before opening links.

Urgent Messages

Be careful with messages threatening account closure or penalties.

Unknown Senders

Verify unexpected messages using an official contact method.

Personal Information

Never share passwords or OTPs through suspicious messages.

Example of a Phishing Message

Subject: Urgent! Your account will be blocked.

Your account needs verification immediately. Click the link below to continue.

Warning: This example uses a fictional message. Do not follow unexpected links or provide account details.

Why is it suspicious?

  • It creates fear and urgency.
  • It asks the recipient to take immediate action.
  • The sender and link need independent verification.

How to Stay Safe

  • Check the sender's email address carefully.
  • Verify links before visiting websites.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs with anyone.
  • Use multi-factor authentication.
  • Keep your browser and security software updated.
  • Report suspicious messages to the appropriate organization.
  • If unsure, visit the official website directly.
